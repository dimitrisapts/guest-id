01

Website Architecture

Three routes. One coherent experience. The homepage creates desire. The partner routes qualify and convert. Everything else is noise.

Route Map

Route /

Homepage

The cinematic entry point. Establishes atmosphere, communicates the vision, and funnels visitors to the two partner paths. No product features. No pricing. Just the world of Island Key.

Route /hosts

Host Journey

Landing page + multi-step application flow. Tone: respectful, professional, aspirational. The host should feel chosen — not sold to. Ends with a qualified application.

Route /operators

Operator Journey

Landing page + multi-step application flow. Tone: collegial, direct, peer-to-peer. The operator should feel like they're joining a network of trusted professionals. Ends with a qualified application.

Shared Infrastructure

Navigation

Minimal persistent nav

  • Logo (top-left) — links to homepage
  • Two ghost links (top-right): "For Hosts" · "For Operators"
  • Nav fades in after hero scroll — invisible on landing
  • On mobile: nav hidden entirely, accessed via hamburger if needed
Footer

Minimal footer

  • Wordmark (left)
  • Email: bookings@islandkey.gr
  • Instagram (when live)
  • © 2026 Island Key · Chania, Crete
  • No sitemap, no cookie banner (handled separately)

Page Weights

Homepage: 6–8 sections · ~3 full-screen moments · target load <3s on 4G
Host/Operator pages: 2 sections + multi-step form · lightweight JS
No blog, no news, no FAQ at this stage — all deferred to post-pilot
Guest access (/guests) deferred — confirmed out of scope.

03

Section-by-Section Content Direction

Every line of copy earns its place. The voice is confident, economical, and specific. It never explains too much. It trusts the visitor to connect the dots.

"Write as if you don't need to convince anyone. The right partners will recognise it immediately."

Voice Rules

What Island Key sounds like

  • Short sentences. Full stops. Confidence.
  • Never explain what could be shown.
  • Avoid: "platform", "solution", "seamless", "curated" (overused)
  • Use: "access", "trusted", "local", "yours", "the island"
  • "Mediterranean" — never "Aegean"
  • Provider names never mentioned. Ever.
  • Copy for hosts ≠ copy for operators. Different register.
Hierarchy

Copy levels per section

  • EYEBROW — 3–5 words, uppercase, 0.6rem, gold
  • HEADLINE — 4–8 words max. Fraunces. Statement not question.
  • BODY — 2–3 sentences absolute maximum per section
  • CTA — verb + noun. "Apply to host →" not "Learn more"
  • NEVER use "click here", "get started", "find out more"

UI/UX Recommendations

Typography System

Two fonts. Total restraint.

  • Fraunces — weight 200–300, display only. Headlines, pull quotes, wordmark. Use italic sparingly for editorial emphasis. Never in body.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans — weight 200–400. All body copy, labels, forms, buttons. Weight 300 as default. Weight 400 for emphasis only.
  • Scale: display 3–6rem · headline 1.4–2.2rem · body 0.85–1rem · label 0.65rem tracking 0.25em uppercase
  • Line-height: display 1.05 · body 1.75 · labels 1.2
Colour System

Dark dominant. Cream breathes.

#0e0e0e — Primary BG
#1B2D4F — Navy mid
#FDFCFA — Cream / text on dark
#C4A55A — Gold / accent
#1A8A7D — Teal / CTA only
#D4854A — Terra / warm accent

Use: dark 70% · cream 20% · gold 8% · teal 2%. Teal only for submit/primary CTA. Gold for eyebrows, underlines, decorative rules. Terra sparingly for warmth in photo sections.

Component Principles

Buttons

One style. Two weights.

  • Primary: teal background, cream text, no border-radius (or very subtle: 2px max)
  • Ghost: transparent, cream border, cream text — for secondary actions
  • Text link: cream text + animated gold underline on hover. Used for CTAs inside dark sections.
  • Never use rounded pill buttons — too casual for this brand.
Forms

Invisible until needed.

  • Input: near-transparent background, 0.5px bottom border only (not full border box)
  • Focus: bottom border becomes gold. No glow, no outline.
  • Label: floats above on focus (material design pattern) — ultra-small uppercase
  • Progress bar: thin 1px gold line, expands left to right. Top of the form area.
  • No box-shadow on any form element.
Images

Always full-bleed or nothing.

  • No rounded corners on any content image
  • Overlay: rgba(0,0,0,0.2–0.35) — never block the image
  • Aspect ratio locked: 16:9 or 3:2 landscape for atmosphere sections
  • No image borders or box shadows
  • Alt text: always present but minimal — "Island coastline, Crete"

Navigation Behaviour

  • On load: nav is invisible. Only the video plays.
  • On first scroll: nav fades in (opacity 0 → 1, 400ms). Logo top-left. Two links top-right: "For Hosts" and "For Operators".
  • On scroll down: nav shrinks slightly (padding reduces). Background: rgba(0,0,0,0.7) backdrop-filter blur.
  • On /hosts and /operators pages: nav always visible from load. Single back arrow or wordmark links back to homepage.
  • No mobile hamburger menu unless needed for /hosts and /operators — these pages are simple enough to not need navigation.

GSAP Usage Guidelines

  • Use GSAP ScrollTrigger for all scroll-based reveals. Set start: "top 80%" — not 100% (too late).
  • Use Lenis for smooth scroll momentum. Configure: lerp: 0.07 for a slow, weighty feel.
  • Avoid Spring physics — they feel too bouncy for this brand. Use ease: "power3.out" throughout.
  • No scroll-jacking on the homepage. Smooth scroll only — never override vertical position.
  • Video: no JS fade — use CSS opacity transition on the video element triggered by canplay event.
  • Reduce motion: add @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) — disable all transforms, keep opacity fades only.

Conversion Strategy

The tension: conversion and premium positioning are usually in conflict. A high-converting site uses urgency, CTAs, social proof, and friction reduction. A premium experience uses restraint, trust, and atmosphere. Island Key leans premium — but conversion still matters. The strategy is to remove every unnecessary step while preserving every atmospheric moment.

"The right partner will convert themselves. Our job is to not lose them to friction or confusion."

Conversion Funnel

Arrive
Visitor lands on homepage via referral, outreach, or email. First impression: cinematic, credible, not a startup landing page.
↓
Feel
Video hero + atmosphere sections establish the world. S2 statement makes them stop. They understand what Island Key IS without reading a features list.
↓
Identify
S5/S6 split sections. Visitor self-identifies: "I'm a host" or "I'm an operator." Single CTA per section. Clear destination.
↓
Trust
Landing page for their path (/hosts or /operators) reinforces what makes Island Key worth their time. Minimal. Confident. No fluff.
↓
Apply
5-step questionnaire. Mobile-optimised. One question visible at a time (card flow). Progress visible. 5 minutes maximum.
↓
Convert
Submit → thank-you moment + confirmation email + Spyros notified on WhatsApp + email. Review within 48 hours.
↓
Refer
Success screen: "Know someone else who should join?" Subtle referral prompt — not a share button blast. A single pre-composed WhatsApp share link.

Conversion Levers (Premium-Compatible)

Do These

Friction reducers

  • One CTA per section — never two competing actions
  • Form: one question at a time (conversational UI reduces abandonment by 40%+)
  • Mobile first: 90% of visitors will be on phone
  • Pre-fill where possible (URL params for referral context)
  • Autofocus first field on each step
  • WhatsApp as primary contact (local trust signal)
  • 48-hour response promise on success screen
  • "Applications reviewed individually" — signals exclusivity, not bureaucracy
Avoid These

Conversion tactics that break premium

  • No "Limited spots available" urgency copy
  • No countdown timers
  • No social proof widgets ("12 people applied today")
  • No popup overlays or exit-intent interruptions
  • No chat widget (unless WhatsApp business — and even then, subtle)
  • No star ratings or review aggregators on the site
  • No auto-playing sound
  • No "FREE" anywhere — commission model doesn't need it

Referral Mechanic

On the success screen after submission, include a single line:
"If you know a property or local business that belongs in this network, share this link with them."

Below it: a pre-composed WhatsApp share link with the text: "I just applied to Island Key — a new concierge platform launching in Crete. Thought you might be interested: www.islandkey.gr"

No buttons. No share count. Just a quiet invitation. This matches the "invitation culture" brief perfectly while generating organic referral growth.
